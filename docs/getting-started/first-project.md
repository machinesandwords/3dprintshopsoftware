# Your First Project

Create and track your first print job from start to finish.

## Step 1: Open the Jobs Sheet

Navigate to the "Jobs" worksheet. You'll see columns for:

- Job ID (auto-generated)
- Customer Name
- Project Description
- Material Type
- Print Time (hours)
- Machine Used
- Status

## Step 2: Enter Job Details

1. Click the first empty row under the headers
2. Fill in:
   - **Customer Name**: Who is this print for?
   - **Project Description**: What are you printing?
   - **Material Type**: PLA, ABS, Resin, etc.
   - **Print Time**: Estimated hours
   - **Machine Used**: Which printer handled this?

3. Press Enter to move to the next row

## Step 3: Track Progress

As your job progresses, update the **Status** column:

- `In Queue` → `Printing` → `Post-Processing` → `Completed` → `Shipped`

The system auto-calculates completion percentage based on elapsed time.

## Step 4: View Costing Data

Switch to the "Costing" sheet to see:

- **Material Cost**: Based on filament type and weight
- **Machine Cost**: Hourly rate × print time
- **Labor Cost**: Post-processing hours × labor rate
- **Total Job Cost**
- **Suggested Retail Price** (auto-calculated with markup)

## Step 5: Generate Quote

Once your job is complete, the system can export a professional quote for your records.

## Tips

- Use consistent material names (they auto-complete)
- Update times regularly for accurate trending
- Review the "Risk Flags" sheet weekly to catch problem customers
